Decision framework

How the score is determined

  • Memorability checks how easy the name is to remember and repeat.
  • Audience fit measures whether the name and mark match the intended market.
  • Distinctiveness rewards concepts that avoid generic category language.
  • Logo scalability prioritizes marks that stay readable from app icon to billboard.
